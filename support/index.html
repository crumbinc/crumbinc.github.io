Help with Crumb

Most questions are answered below. If yours is not, write to us.

Write to us or report a problem

Email appsupport@outbox.au. Include your Crumb version (in Me, Settings, About Crumb) and your Android version.

In the app, Me, Settings, About Crumb, Report a problem opens an email where you tick what goes in: your Crumb version, your Android version, your phone model and the last crash report saved on your phone. Nothing is added unless you tick it, and your diary is never added.

When you report a problem, a screenshot and the steps that led to it help a lot.

Never send your diary or a backup file. We never need them.

Backups and moving

Crumb keeps everything on your phone and has no account, so your backup file is your safety net. Read the full backup and restore guide, including moving from an earlier test version.

How do I back up?

Go to Me, Backup and data, and tap Create backup. Crumb saves one file with your foods, recipes, diary, plans, goals, settings and photos. Keep it somewhere safe.

The file is not locked. Keep it private. A synced folder sends a copy to that cloud service.

Want a spreadsheet instead? Export CSV makes a zip with your diary, energy, weight, waist, water and caffeine. A CSV is for reading, not for restoring.

Can Crumb back up by itself?

Yes. Under Scheduled backup, choose Daily or Weekly, then pick a folder and how many files to keep. Back up now runs one straight away. If Crumb loses access to the folder, it tells you and asks you to choose it again.

How do I restore?

In Me, Backup and data, tap Restore from file and choose your backup. Backups from older versions of Crumb work too. Crumb shows what is inside, then asks how to restore:

  • Replace deletes what is in Crumb now and uses the file. A copy of your current data is kept until you close Crumb, so you can undo.
  • Merge adds what is missing and keeps what is here. Goals and settings come from the file.
How do I move to a new phone?
  1. On the old phone, create a backup in Me, Backup and data.
  2. Copy the file to the new phone. A USB cable, a cloud drive, email to yourself or Quick Share all work.
  3. Install Crumb on the new phone and open it.
  4. On the first setup step, tap Restore from a backup and choose the file.

Android's own phone-to-phone copy will not carry your diary, because Crumb switches Android's cloud backup off. Use the backup file instead.

Moving your diary like this earns the Moving day sticker.

Food data

Does Crumb work offline?

Mostly. The Australian part of Open Food Facts and the Australian plain foods list come with the app, so scanning and searching them work without signal. Looking up a product that is not on the phone, importing a recipe from a link and downloading updates need internet.

In Me, Settings, Offline food data, you can keep only the products you open, add the most popular ones, or download every Open Food Facts product sold in the countries you choose. Turn on Wi-Fi only to keep big downloads off mobile data. Keep products up to date is on from the start: Crumb fetches new products each week and follows your Wi-Fi only choice. Nothing about you is sent.

Removing offline products keeps the ones you favourited, logged, used in a recipe or meal, or edited.

Why is a product missing or wrong?

Product data is made by volunteers at Open Food Facts and can be wrong or missing. Check the label. You can add your own food in Crumb, and you can fix the product on Open Food Facts for everyone.

Permissions

What does Crumb ask for, and why?

Every permission is optional. See and change them all in Me, Settings, Permissions.

  • Camera: scan barcodes and labels.
  • Notifications: reminders, the weekly summary, cook timers and download progress. Off until you turn them on.
  • Exact alarms: lets reminders arrive on the minute. Without it a reminder can be a few minutes late.
  • Battery: stops the phone delaying reminders.
  • Microphone: voice search, only if you switch it on.

If Android says a permission is turned off, tap Open settings on that row and turn it on there.

My reminders are late or do not arrive

Some phone makers stop apps in the background to save battery. In Me, Settings, Permissions, open the Battery row. On Samsung, keep Crumb out of Sleeping apps. On Nothing phones, set Crumb's battery to Unrestricted. Xiaomi, OPPO, vivo and Huawei have their own switch, and the row names it for your phone.

Files and display

A file will not open in Crumb

Some file managers, such as the one on the Nothing Phone (3a), give a file no type or the wrong one. Crumb ignores that and looks inside the file to see what it is.

  • Recipe files and offline product packs: tap the file and choose Crumb, or use Share and pick Crumb. You can also use Me, Backup and data, Import files.
  • Backups never open from outside Crumb, on purpose. Use Restore from file inside the app.
  • If Crumb says it is not a recipe file, the file is something else. Check you picked the right one.
Can I hide the numbers?

Yes. In Me, Settings, Display and units, switch on Hide numbers. Crumb shows words and progress shapes instead of calories, kilojoules and weight. You can switch it off again any time.

Still stuck?

Email appsupport@outbox.au with your Crumb and Android versions. Crumb is a small independent project, so replies can take a few days.
