Features

What Crumb does

Crumb is a free food diary for Android. These are the features in the current beta on Google Play.

Diary

  • Breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks, or rename, add, reorder and hide your own sections.
  • A day total ring for energy and water, or one slim line instead.
  • Choose which Diary cards show and in what order.
  • A Nutrition view for the day: macros, limits, share of energy, vitamins and minerals.
  • A full nutrition table for any day, against the label reference or your own goals.
  • A banner when a nutrient you chose, such as sodium or caffeine, nears your daily limit.
  • A Last 7 days card that opens your Overview.
  • Tap the date at the top to go to any day, and log past days.
  • Hold an entry to edit it, move or copy it to another meal, or delete it.
  • Add a photo to any entry.
  • Tap a total to see the foods behind it, and which ones have no figure for it.

Adding and scanning food

  • Food search that forgives typos and works offline for foods already on your phone.
  • Optional voice search, using your phone's own speech service.
  • Scan a barcode. Australian products come with the app, so it works with no signal.
  • Photograph a nutrition panel and Crumb reads it on your phone.
  • Scan an ingredients list, with pack statements kept word for word.
  • Add a missing product with photos of its barcode, front, panel and ingredients.
  • Crumb asks for more photos of the pack when a label is hard to read, or every time if you like.
  • A heads-up when a photo is blurry, too dark or has glare, so you can retake it.
  • Optional Document scan straightens and cleans up a label photo first.
  • Scan from a photo already on your phone, not only the camera.
  • Advanced and off by default: read labels online with your own Google Cloud Vision key.
  • Plain foods like flour, onions and chicken from the Australian Food Composition Database.
  • Log in grams, millilitres, serves, spoons, cups or pieces.
  • Quick add: an energy figure on its own, with protein, carbs and fat if you want.
  • Favourites, your own foods, recipes and meals, all in one place when you add food.
  • Suggestions of what you usually have at this meal.
  • Copy a meal you logged in the last 14 days.
  • One tap logs your last portion or the food's default portion.
  • Create your own food, with a short form or every field at once.
  • Import a menu item from a web link, pasted text or a screenshot.
  • Product pages with a nutrition table, per serve or per 100 g.
  • Flags from the pack for your allergens, for vegetarian or vegan, and for words you choose.
  • Products with caffeine or added sugars above levels you set are flagged.
  • Private notes on products and recipes, kept on your phone.
  • Share a product as a picture card, QR code, file or text.
  • Scan a Crumb QR code or open a Crumb file to add a shared product or recipe.
  • Share a web page, label photo or barcode picture from another app into Crumb.

Recipes and meals

  • Write recipes with photos, serves, ingredients, method steps and headers, tags and a source.
  • Import a recipe from a web link, or share the page to Crumb.
  • Import from pasted text, photos of a cookbook page or a PDF, read on your phone.
  • Open Crumb, Paprika, Mealie and Tandoor recipe files, or a saved recipe page.
  • Imported ingredients are matched to foods, and Crumb remembers the ones you pick.
  • Every recipe gets its own nutrition label, worked out from its ingredients.
  • Save meals you eat again, like your usual breakfast, with no method needed.
  • Sort recipes and meals into folders and tags.
  • Keep versions of a recipe or meal and switch between them.
  • Log what you ate by serves, percent, grams or each ingredient.
  • Change the ingredients you ate, then keep the change for today only, save it to the recipe, or save it as a new version.
  • Drink recipes, logged by the millilitre.
  • Cook mode with big buttons: one step at a time, with that step's ingredients.
  • Cook for more or fewer serves and every amount scales to match.
  • See any ingredient amount in other units.
  • Cook timers that keep counting and ring with the screen off.
  • When you finish cooking, log a serve straight to the Diary.
  • Scan a product and add it straight into a recipe.
  • Share a recipe as a picture card, QR code, Crumb file, PDF or Word file.

Plan and shopping

  • Plan meals week by week, for any day up to a year ahead.
  • Repeat a planned meal every week, until a date if you like.
  • Copy the meals from one week into another.
  • A Calendar with month and agenda views of what you ate and planned.
  • A shopping list added up from your planned meals over 7, 14 or 30 days.
  • Tap an item to see which planned meals need it.
  • Add a recipe's ingredients, your own items, or scan a barcode to add it.
  • The same item adds up instead of repeating. Tick off and clear as you shop.
  • Share the shopping list to another app.

Water and caffeine

  • A Water card where one tap adds a glass, in the size you choose.
  • A daily water target, set for you or typed in yourself.
  • Count other drinks you log towards water, choose how much of each counts, and leave out alcohol if you like.
  • Water reminders between hours you set, which stop once you reach your target.
  • A Caffeine card with a daily limit and a step size you choose.
  • Caffeine in the foods and drinks you log is counted for you.
  • Show water in cups and caffeine in coffees, if you prefer.

Goals and body

  • A daily energy goal estimated from your body profile, using the Schofield or Mifflin-St Jeor equation.
  • Protein, carbs and fat targets, plus fibre, vitamins and minerals.
  • Daily limits for sodium, sugars, saturated fat and caffeine, which you can change.
  • Log your weight and see a smoothed trend that evens out daily ups and downs.
  • An optional waist log with its own chart.
  • Optional BMI, with a range for your age or background where one is published.
  • Set a weight goal and pace, and see an estimated arrival date.
  • Crumb checks your maintenance energy against your weigh-ins and suggests updates.
  • Pregnancy and breastfeeding settings pause weight goals and adjust daily targets.
  • Pounds, stone, and feet and inches, if you prefer them.
  • An Overview by day, week, month, year or your own dates, with an energy chart.
  • See your most eaten meals and how many days landed near your goal.
  • An optional weekly summary notification, on the day and time you pick.
  • An optional weigh-in reminder.

Stickers

  • Up to three daily stickers: All meals logged, Energy within range and Water goal met.
  • Daily stickers show on the Diary week strip and the Calendar.
  • Every sticker you earn stays on your Stickers page.
  • Optional week-long challenges, with three new ones offered each day.
  • Milestone stickers, with a pop-up, a quiet note or no notice at all.
  • Choose whether the energy sticker counts against your goal or your maintenance.
  • Share a sticker, day, week or meal as a card, numbers hidden unless you choose.

Backup, privacy and data

  • No account, no ads, no analytics and no Crumb server. Your diary stays on your phone.
  • In-app pages that explain how each figure is worked out, where the data comes from, and what is stored and sent.
  • Back up everything to a file you keep. Crumb never uploads it.
  • Scheduled daily or weekly backups to a folder you pick.
  • Restore on a new phone with every setting in place.
  • Export your diary, energy, weight, waist, water and caffeine as CSV files.
  • Import many recipe, product and meal files at once, or a zip, with undo.
  • Keep the products you have opened, the most popular ones, or every product for the countries you choose, on your phone.
  • Weekly product updates, on Wi-Fi only if you like.
  • Load an Open Food Facts data file you already downloaded.
  • Take the products you have opened to another phone, as a file.

Settings and accessibility

  • Light, off-white, dim, dark and black themes, or follow your phone's light or dark setting.
  • Energy in kilojoules or calories, your choice.
  • Metric, imperial or both, with Australian, US or UK spoon and cup sizes.
  • Show amounts in cups and spoons, or only in grams and millilitres.
  • Label-style rounding, or choose how many decimal places to show.
  • Show nutrition per serve or per 100 g, everywhere at once.
  • Hide numbers: see words and progress shapes instead of energy and weight figures.
  • Turn off any feature you do not use. What you logged is kept for when you turn it back on.
  • Meal reminders that skip a meal you have already logged.
  • One Permissions page for camera, microphone, notifications, alarms and battery.
  • A short setup on first launch, which you can run again at any time.
  • Works with large text sizes and screen readers.
  • Layouts for tablets and foldables.
