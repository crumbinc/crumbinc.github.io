Legal

Health and legal notice

The same notice Crumb asks you to read and accept when you start. Notice dated 17 November 2026, version 2.

General information only

Crumb is a food diary and a calculator. What it shows is general information about food and energy. It is not medical, dietary, or lifestyle advice. It is not a medical device, and it is not made to diagnose, treat, cure, prevent, manage, or monitor any condition, or to assess your risk of one. Nothing in Crumb takes the place of advice from someone who knows you and your health.

Ages

Crumb is for adults aged 18 and over. It asks for your date of birth when you start, and it does not open for anyone younger. By accepting this notice you confirm that the date of birth you give is correct.

Talk to a professional first

See a doctor or an accredited practising dietitian before you change what you eat, your weight, or how much you move. That matters most if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, living with a medical condition, taking medication, or have ever had a hard time with food or an eating disorder. If you are unsure, ask. Never put off or ignore professional advice because of something you saw in Crumb.

Targets are not advice

Crumb does not want to push anyone towards unhealthy habits. Targets, limits, stickers, and the lowest energy figure Crumb will suggest are tools, not advice about what you should eat, and not goals to chase at the cost of your health. You can set, change, or ignore the targets, limits, and stickers. The lowest energy figure is built into Crumb and cannot be changed. Eating less is not always better, and hitting every number is not the point. If tracking makes you anxious, or starts to feel like a rule you cannot break, stop and talk to someone (see Where to get help).

Figures are estimates

Every figure in Crumb is an estimate and can be wrong. Open Food Facts is filled in by volunteers, so a product can be out of date or have a mistake. Labels round their numbers. A barcode or label scan can misread. Plain food values are averages, so your apple or your loaf will differ. Conversions between cups, spoons, grams, and millilitres, and the densities behind them, are approximate. A product or recipe someone shares with you carries whatever they typed. Energy goals, weight trends, projections, and BMI come from formulas that fit some people better than others, because bodies differ. Me, Settings, About Crumb, How Crumb works explains each calculation. Check anything that matters against the pack or with a professional.

Not for allergy or food safety decisions

Allergen, ingredient, and "may contain" details come from volunteers, from your own entries, or from a label photo that Crumb reads on your phone (or that Google Cloud Vision reads with your own key, if you turn on Read labels online). They can be wrong or missing. The My allergens flags are Crumb's own match of the words you chose against the pack's Contains line and ingredients list. A "may contain" line, and words elsewhere on the pack, never make a flag, and a food with no flag can still contain an allergen. Never rely on Crumb for an allergy, intolerance, or food safety decision. Read the pack in your hand and follow your doctor's advice.

Reference values suit healthy adults

The daily reference values, the BMI ranges, and the other guides in Crumb are set for generally healthy adults. They may not suit older people, anyone pregnant or breastfeeding, athletes, or anyone with a medical condition. Your own needs can be different.

Where to get help

In an emergency, call 000.

For urgent emotional support, call Lifeline on 13 11 14. For support with eating, food, or body image, call the Butterfly National Helpline on 1800 33 4673 (8am to midnight every day except national public holidays) or use its webchat at butterfly.org.au. It is not a crisis line. Your doctor can help too.

As is, and your rights

Nothing in this notice limits any right you have under the Australian Consumer Law or any other law that cannot be excluded. Subject to that, Crumb and its data are provided without any promise that they are accurate or complete, so check anything that matters against the pack or with a professional.

That is the end of the notice. How Crumb handles your data is on the privacy policy page.
