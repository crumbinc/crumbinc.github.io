Troubleshooting

What to do when reminders are late, a scan finds nothing, a backup will not open, a download waits, or a feature is missing.

On this page

  • Reminders arrive late or not at all
  • A scan does not find the product
  • A backup will not open
  • A scheduled backup stops
  • A download or update waits
  • A screen or button is missing
  • Report a problem
  • Related guides

Most problems in Crumb come down to a phone setting, a connection, or a file. Find the message you see below, then follow its steps.

Reminders arrive late or not at all

Some phones stop apps in the background to save battery.

  1. Open Me, Settings, Permissions.
  2. Check Notifications. If it is not allowed, tap the row and allow it.
  3. Check Exact alarms. Without it, a reminder or cook timer can arrive a few minutes late. Tap the row to allow alarms and reminders.
  4. Check Battery. Tap the row so the phone stops delaying Crumb's reminders.
  5. Under Battery, a row for your phone's maker names the setting to change. On Samsung it is to keep Crumb out of Sleeping apps. On Nothing, it is to set Crumb's battery to Unrestricted. Tap the row to open that setting. If it cannot open, look under Battery, then Crumb, in Android Settings.

A meal reminder is skipped when that meal is already in your diary. The water reminder stops once your goal is met and while the Diary is open. The weigh-in reminder is skipped on days you logged your weight.

A scan does not find the product

  • Not in Crumb yet. Neither Open Food Facts nor this phone has the barcode. Tap Add this product, or Scan again.
  • Is this the barcode on the pack? Crumb may have misread it. Tap Scan again, or Type it and fix the digits.
  • You are offline. Connect and try again. Crumb checks again when you are back online.
  • Could not reach Open Food Facts. Tap Try again. If it is busy, wait a minute.
  • No barcode found. In a picture from your photos, the bars were not sharp or not fully in view. Tap Another photo.
  • Camera is off for Crumb. Turn it on in Me, Settings, Permissions, or tap Search instead. If it is on but black, close any other app using the camera and tap Try again.

Product data comes from Open Food Facts volunteers and can be wrong or missing. Check the label.

A backup will not open

  1. Open it from inside Crumb: Me, Backup and data, Restore from file, then choose the file. Tapping a .loaf file in Files, Drive, email, or a chat app also offers Crumb, which shows what it holds before anything is saved.
  2. That file is not a Crumb backup, or it is damaged. Check you picked the backup, not a CSV export or a recipe file. Backups end in .loaf; Crumb does not open older backup names.
  3. This backup was made by a newer version of Crumb. Update Crumb in Google Play, then restore again.
  4. This backup has a recipe Crumb cannot open. The message names the recipe. Nothing was changed. Use Report a problem with Crumb version ticked, so we can look.
  5. Something went wrong. Try again. If it keeps happening, report it.

Every one of these says Nothing was changed, so your data is as it was. On a new phone, use Restore from a backup on the first setup step.

A scheduled backup stops

  • Crumb can no longer write to the folder. Under the schedule, choose Folder again.
  • Last backup failed. Crumb tries again at the next time. Tap Back up now to try straight away.

A download or update waits

In Me, Settings, Offline food data, Offline products, the update line says what it waits for:

  • Update waits for Wi-Fi. Connect, or tap Use mobile data for this one update.
  • Update waits for a connection. It starts when you are online.
  • Update is queued. Android starts it when the phone allows background work.
  • Update will try again. Open Food Facts is busy.

Your saved products keep working. A message about not enough storage means you should free some space.

A screen or button is missing

Open Me, Settings, Features and check the feature is on. For a file that will not import, a read that fails, or a key that is not accepted, see Moving to a new phone, Import a recipe, and Read labels online.

Report a problem

Open Me, Settings, About Crumb, Report a problem. Tick what to add: Crumb version, Android version, Phone model, or The last crash report. Tap Continue and send the email. Do not send your diary or a backup file.
