Goals and energy

How Crumb works out a daily energy figure, how to set your own targets, and how to hide the numbers.

  • Related guides

Me, Goals and body holds your daily energy figure and every other target, and shows where each one comes from.

Fill in your body profile

  1. In Me, Goals and body, tap Body profile.
  2. Choose Female or Male under About you, then enter Born, Height, and Weight. A new weight is added as today's weigh-in.
  3. Choose your Activity: Sedentary, Light, Moderate, Active, or Very active.
  4. Tap Save. It stays on this phone.

How Crumb works out daily energy

Until your profile is filled in, the daily energy is 8,700 kJ, the FSANZ reference used on Australian nutrition panels.

With your details, Crumb estimates your resting energy, then multiplies it by a number for your activity. Under Energy calculation, you pick the equation:

  • Schofield is the equation set behind NHMRC's Eat for Health tables. Crumb starts with it. It uses age band and sex, not height.
  • Mifflin-St Jeor is common in calculators outside Australia. It uses exact age and height.

Both are estimates, and they can differ. To change, open the Energy section and tap one. A message offers Undo. If you typed your own daily energy, Change your own targets? asks first. How your energy is worked out opens the full explanation.

Set your own targets

  1. Open a section: Energy, Protein, carbs, and fat, Water, Vitamins and minerals, or Limits.
  2. Type a figure. A field marked Auto follows your profile. One you typed shows Set by you.
  3. Tap Save goals. To put one back, tap Use Auto under it.

For protein, carbs, and fat, choose Grams or Percent of energy. The percentages must add up to 100. A target you set is never changed by your profile. If a change would move one, Crumb asks first.

Hide the numbers

  1. In Me, Settings, Display and units, turn on Hide numbers.
  2. Energy, weight, and BMI show as words and shapes, such as About halfway and Near goal.
  3. Daily energy shows only Auto or Set by you. To change it, tap Change, type a number, tap Set, then Save goals.

Crumb still keeps every figure. Turn it off to see them again.

Good to know

  • Energy shows in kJ or kcal, as set in Energy unit. Crumb keeps kJ, and 1 kcal is 4.184 kJ.
  • With a weight goal, the Energy section shows Maintenance energy and today's target.
  • Weekly maintenance check lets Crumb suggest a new maintenance figure from your logged days and weigh-ins. Nothing changes until you tap Update targets.
  • Reset to defaults puts every goal back to Auto or its starting figure. Your diary does not change.
  • Me, Settings, About Crumb, How Crumb works lists every source and equation.

Questions

Which equation should I pick? Crumb does not choose for you. Schofield matches the Australian tables. Mifflin-St Jeor counts your height. You can switch at any time.

I typed my own daily energy. Will a new weigh-in change it? No. It stays until you tick it in Change your own targets? or tap Use Auto.
