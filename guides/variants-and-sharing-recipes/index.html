Variants and sharing recipes

Keep versions of a recipe together as variants of a Base recipe, and share a recipe or meal as a picture card, a QR code, or a file.

  • Other ways to manage variants
  • Share a recipe or a meal

  • Questions
  • Related guides

A variant is a changed copy of a recipe, such as a vegetarian one, kept with the recipe it came from. That first recipe is the Base recipe.

Make a variant

  1. Open the recipe. On the Recipes tab, tap the recipe. The Variant field under its name shows which one is on screen.
  2. Start the variant. Tap the Variant field, then New variant from this. The editor opens on a copy named Variant 2.
  3. Change what differs. Rename it, swap ingredients, or change steps. Tap Save recipe.
  4. Switch between them. Tap the Variant field and pick one.

Other ways to manage variants

  • Duplicate. In the recipe menu, tap Duplicate, then choose Separate copy or Variant of this. The copy opens ready to save in the same folder. Back drops it.
  • Merge as variants. On Recipes, open the menu, tap Select, tick two or more, then Merge as variants. Pick the base. Crumb asks what happens to Favourites and pins and Folders, and offers Undo. Nothing is deleted.
  • Split. On a variant, tap Split from variants, or Detach from base recipe in Edit recipe. It keeps everything it has.
  • Delete the Base recipe. Its variants stay as recipes of their own, and its planned meals leave the plan. Diary entries keep their numbers.

Share a recipe or a meal

  1. Open the share sheet. On the recipe page, tap the share button, or Share recipe in the menu. How do you want to share it? lists the ways.
  2. Picture card. Choose the Style (Clean, Bold, or Scrapbook), Light or Dark where the style has both, the Shape, and Show numbers, which adds a nutrition table. The card shows the total time and fits its content.
  3. QR code. Someone scans it with Crumb to add the recipe. Under Share as, pick Picture only or Card, then Save or share. If the recipe is too large, Crumb says so and offers to share the file instead.
  4. File. The File sheet offers a Crumb file (.crumble, with its photo), a PDF, and a Word document, each with a Save button beside it (see Recipe PDFs).
  5. Meals. A saved meal shares the same ways.

Good to know

  • Nothing leaves your phone until you pick where to send it.
  • A shared recipe is a copy. Later changes do not reach the other person.
  • A file can leave out photos to stay small. The sheet says how many.
  • To import what someone sent you, see Import a recipe.

Questions

Does a variant change when I edit the Base recipe? No. Each variant is its own recipe with its own ingredients and steps.

Can someone open my QR code without Crumb? A phone camera can scan it, but only Crumb can read it.
