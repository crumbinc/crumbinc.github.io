Settings tour

Every page in Crumb's Settings, what it holds, and where to find it.

Settings has its rows in three groups, and each row opens one page. Open it from Me, Settings.

How Crumb looks and works

Features. Me, Settings, Features turns off what you do not use. A feature you turn off goes from every screen, and your data stays. Switches are grouped as Body, Food and nutrition, Drinks, Recipes and planning, Adding food, and Rewards and sharing. Some need another switch first, and the row says so. At least one thing to track stays on.

Display and units. Sets how Crumb looks and the units it shows.

  • Appearance: Same as phone, or a fixed theme: Light, Off-white, Dark, Dim, or Black.
  • Units: Hide numbers, Energy unit, Measures, Spoon and cup sizes, Show cups and spoons, Round decimals, and Label-style panels.
  • Nutrition: Show nutrition per and the basis for percentages.
  • Recipes: how folders are shown and ordered, and Oven temperatures in recipes.
  • Calendar: Newest day first.
  • Messages: Where messages show (Top or Bottom) and Time left to Undo (Bar or Ring).

Diary and trackers. Diary sections and Diary layout (see Diary sections your way), One-tap amount, Drinks count as water, Include alcoholic drinks, Cups and coffees (see Water and caffeine), Full food form, Separate scan rows, Voice search, Always take extra photos, and Stickers (see Stickers explained).

Limits and warnings. A Daily limits row points to Me, Goals and body. Warnings holds six switches. Warn me about turns off one nutrient's warnings. My allergens and diet and My additives set what packs are checked against (see Warnings and allergens).

Reminders. One page for Meal reminders, Remind me to drink water, the weigh-in reminder, Send a weekly summary, and Challenge reminders. Reminders need notifications on (see Troubleshooting).

Data and phone

Offline food data. Offline products, How many to keep, and Products you searched or scanned (see Offline food data).

Read labels online. Advanced, off to start (see Read labels online).

Backup and data. The same page as Me, Backup and data (see Moving to a new phone).

Permissions. Shows Camera, Notifications, Microphone, Exact alarms, and Battery, each allowed or not. Tap a row to allow it, or to open the phone's settings.

Crumb

Run setup again. Opens the setup steps. It changes settings only and deletes nothing.

About Crumb. The version is under the name. It holds Updates, How Crumb works, Health and legal notice, Privacy, Sources and licences, Report a problem, Last crash report, a link to rate Crumb on Google Play, and On the website links: Features, What has changed, Backup and restore guide, Support, and Delete your data.

Good to know

  • Each page is two taps from the Me tab: Settings, then the page.
  • A page or row whose feature is off in Features leaves Settings.
  • Settings are in your backup.
  • Goals, the water goal, and limits are in Me, Goals and body (see Goals and energy).

Questions

I cannot find Read labels online. It shows only while Scan labels is on in Me, Settings, Features.

How do I go back to the default Diary layout? In Me, Settings, Diary and trackers, Diary layout, tap Reset layout.

Where is the energy calculation? In Me, Goals and body, the Energy section.
