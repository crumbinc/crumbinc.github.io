Privacy

Delete your Crumb data

How to delete what Crumb holds about you.

  1. Delete everything on your phone

    Crumb keeps your diary, recipes, weight, water, photos and settings only on your phone. To delete all of it, open Android Settings, Apps, Crumb, Storage, Clear storage (Clear data on Samsung), or uninstall Crumb.

  2. Delete your backup files

    Backup files and CSV exports you saved are yours. Delete them where you saved them.

  3. Delete your emails to us

    If you emailed us, write to appsupport@outbox.au and ask us to delete your emails. We reply within 30 days.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • On your phone. Everything is deleted at once when you clear storage or uninstall.
  • Accounts and servers. Crumb has no account and no server copy of your data, so there is nothing else of yours for us to delete.
  • Support emails. Kept for 12 months, then deleted, or sooner if you ask.
  • Searches and label photos. Searches sent to Open Food Facts, and label photos sent to Google Cloud Vision (only if Read labels online was on), are not kept by Crumb. Those services have their own policies: Open Food Facts and Google Cloud Vision.

The full details are in section 16 of the privacy policy, “How long we keep data, and deleting it”.
